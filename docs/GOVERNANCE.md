# AURA Responsible AI & Governance

## Autonomous actions
- Order status lookup
- Tracking/status communication
- Standard ticket updates
- Standard compensation within configured threshold and policy

## Human approval
- Compensation above threshold
- Policy exceptions
- Legal/regulatory/safety cases
- Fraud suspicion
- Low-confidence cases
- High-risk customer situations

## Audit fields
Case ID, input, retrieved evidence, policy used, model, confidence, recommendation, action, approver, timestamp and outcome.

## Pre-production controls
SSO/RBAC, PII minimization, secrets management, retention policy, model evaluation, red-team testing, prompt-injection testing, tool authorization, rate limiting and human shadow mode.
