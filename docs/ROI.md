# AURA ROI Model

All figures are modeled assumptions for the fictional NexaCommerce scenario.

Annual interaction volume = 600,000
Cost per interaction = ₹180
Annual handling-cost pool = 600,000 × ₹180 = ₹10.8 Cr

Scenario benefit = annual cost pool × efficiency improvement

Simple ROI = (annual benefit − annual investment) / annual investment × 100

Payback months = annual investment / annual benefit × 12

Separate business benefits into:
- hard savings
- capacity released
- revenue protection
- customer experience improvement

Do not present modeled benefits as guaranteed cash savings.
