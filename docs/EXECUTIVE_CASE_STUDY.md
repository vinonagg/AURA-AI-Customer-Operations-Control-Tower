# AURA — Executive Case Study

## Business challenge
Customer operations agents repeatedly investigate the same fragmented facts: customer value, previous contacts, order status, shipment exceptions and applicable policies. This creates longer handling time, inconsistent resolutions and limited ability to detect systemic operational causes.

## Proposed solution
AURA combines structured operational data, semantic policy retrieval, agent-style orchestration and a governance gate. The system generates a recommended resolution only after assembling evidence, then decides whether the action is eligible for autonomous execution.

## Value hypothesis
The portfolio scenario models 600,000 annual interactions at ₹180 per interaction. At a 20% efficiency improvement, the modeled annual benefit is ₹2.16 Cr. A ₹60 L annual investment scenario produces a 260% simple ROI and a 3.3-month payback. These are illustrative assumptions, not actual company results.

## Differentiators
1. **Grounded AI:** policy evidence is retrieved before a decision is made.
2. **Agentic workflow:** specialized steps separate intake, investigation, policy, root cause, resolution and governance.
3. **Controlled autonomy:** material or low-confidence decisions route to humans.
4. **Operational intelligence:** case-level evidence feeds root-cause analysis.
5. **Measurable AI:** synthetic evaluation benchmark included.

## Stakeholder outcomes
- Operations: lower investigation effort and standardized resolution pathways.
- Quality: policy-grounded decisions and auditability.
- Technology: modular API-first architecture.
- Risk: explicit human approval boundary.
- Finance: transparent scenario-based value model.

## Production transition
The next enterprise phase would integrate CRM/OMS/TMS systems, enterprise identity, managed storage, observability, PII controls and a formal model-evaluation program.
