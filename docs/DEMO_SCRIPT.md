# AURA Executive Demo Script — 7 Minutes

## 0:00 — Business problem
"Customer operations teams spend significant effort investigating repetitive cases across CRM, order, shipment and policy systems. The problem is not only response generation; it is decision latency, inconsistent policy application and limited root-cause visibility."

## 0:45 — Product
Open the Control Tower and explain that AURA is a decision system: it investigates evidence, grounds the decision in policy, applies governance and then executes or escalates.

## 1:30 — Flagship case
Use C10001 and the delayed-order message. Run AURA.

Point to:
- priority
- confidence
- compensation
- root cause
- trace
- evidence

Say: "Notice that the model is not being trusted blindly. The decision is bounded by policy and an autonomous execution threshold."

## 2:45 — Evidence
Show the policy evidence and shipment details. Explain that the resolution is grounded in customer, order, shipment and policy records.

## 3:30 — Governance
Show the governance tab. Explain autonomous versus human-approved actions. Then demonstrate a higher-value compensation request that crosses the threshold.

## 4:30 — Operational intelligence
Show carrier/hub concentration and the AI recommendation. Move the story from individual ticket automation to systemic operations improvement.

## 5:15 — Economics
Show the ROI scenario. State explicitly that the values are scenario assumptions and must be validated by Finance before investment decisions.

## 5:50 — Evaluation
Run the benchmark and explain that the evaluation is a synthetic portfolio benchmark, not a production accuracy claim.

## 6:20 — Close
"The target state is not an autonomous chatbot. It is governed AI decision infrastructure that can safely compress investigation time, standardize policy decisions and surface operational root causes while keeping humans in control of material risk."
